What is Cholibrium?

Cholibrium is described by its seller as a capsule built from ten medicinal mushroom extracts. It is positioned to support cholesterol that is already within a normal range, healthy circulation, antioxidant protection and steady daily energy, alongside a heart-conscious diet and regular movement.

The idea

A blend instead of one ingredient

The seller says it combines ten mushroom species rather than one isolated nutrient, each contributing different naturally occurring compounds.

The routine

Two capsules a day

The product is described as 60 capsules, a 30-day supply, taken as two capsules with a glass of water 20 to 30 minutes before a meal.

The standards

Quality claims to verify

The seller says it is made in an FDA-registered, cGMP-certified facility, is non-GMO and gluten-free, and is vegan, keto and paleo friendly. FDA registration is not FDA approval.

Worth knowing: Statements about cholesterol, circulation, energy and antioxidants are the seller's, and this guide cannot verify them. Cholibrium is a dietary supplement. It is not meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it should not replace medical advice or prescribed treatment.

The ten mushrooms the seller names

These notes rewrite the seller's descriptions in plain language. They show what the seller says each mushroom is traditionally used for, not effects we have confirmed.

Reishi

A traditional mushroom the seller links with antioxidant compounds and healthy blood vessels.

Lion's Mane

Described as a mushroom of modern research interest for nerve and cognitive wellness.

Cordyceps

Traditionally used for stamina, and described by the seller as relating to oxygen use and cellular energy.

Shiitake

Described as containing eritadenine, a compound the seller says has been studied for fat metabolism.

Maitake

Described as researched for how the body handles dietary fat and cholesterol.

Turkey Tail

Described as rich in polysaccharides and valued for general antioxidant and immune support.

Chaga

Described as high in antioxidants and traditionally used to support the body against oxidative stress.

Royal Agaricus

Also called the mushroom of the sun, and described as adding beta-glucan diversity.

White Button

The common culinary mushroom, described as adding polysaccharides and nutritional variety.

Black Fungus

Also called tree ear, described as a traditional ingredient containing polysaccharides.

Potential advantages and things to question

  • A clear, named list of ten ingredients that is easy to research.
  • A simple routine of two capsules a day.
  • The seller's own guidance stresses diet and activity rather than promising results.
  • A 365-day refund window and no subscription are advertised.
  • Larger packages list a lower per-bottle price, free shipping and three bonus guides.

What to keep in mind

  • Cholesterol, circulation and energy claims are the seller's and are not verified here.
  • The amount of each mushroom per capsule is not given in the supplied text.
  • The seller itself says cardiovascular-related support is judged over at least 90 days, so this is not a quick fix.
  • If you take statins, blood thinners or blood pressure medicine, a supplement needs a conversation with your doctor first.
  • The seller says it is sold only through its own channel, so you cannot compare prices elsewhere.
  • We have not purchased or tested Cholibrium, or checked shipping or how refunds are handled.

Our view: it may suit adults who already eat and move with their heart in mind and want a simple mushroom supplement habit. It is not a replacement for medical care, and it is a poor fit if you take heart or blood pressure medicine and have not asked your doctor, or if you want a guaranteed change in your numbers.
